Trackpad quick start

Select units → hover destination → A attack-move or M move. Hover a unit → F to follow (or right-click a friendly). Or Ctrl/⌘+click to move, Alt+click to attack-move. Pan with arrow keys or minimap while an army is selected.

Radar

A powered Radar shows enemy units and buildings on the minimap anywhere you have already explored (not only in current vision). It does not remove fog on the main map — scout to explore first.

Selection

Multi-unit selection shows a grid in the sidebar. Click a portrait to select only that unit; Ctrl+click toggles units in/out of the group. Orders (move / attack) apply to the current selection.

Multiplayer

Speed changes must be requested then accepted by the opponent (both clients share the server clock). Matches are recorded for Watch replays. Share a recording with ?replay=ID (Copy link on the replays list).
